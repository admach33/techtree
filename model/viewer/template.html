A card counts as unlocked once its year arrives and it is rated High or Medium at the next checkpoint (end-2031 for years through 2031, end-2035 after). The year is the earliest credible year, not a forecast date.

    Likelihood and trust

    Likelihood at two checkpoints
    Each node is rated for the end of 2031 and the end of 2035. Bars show the checkpoint for the chosen year. Four bars: High (roughly over 70%). Three: Medium (30–70%). Two: Low (10–30%). One: Speculative (under 10%). These are analyst judgments, not computed probabilities.
    Earliest credible year
    The first year the node is credibly achieved: a result proved, a tool in use, a first flight. A card cannot unlock before its prerequisites.
    Trust tier of a premise
    T1 a manuscript only, T2 a manuscript with an outside expert's endorsement, T3 an unchecked Lean proof in the lab's repository, T4 an independent machine check plus an audit of the formal statement. Nothing in the October 2026 release is above T3. Nodes resting on T1 or T2 results are conditional.
    Binding gate
    The slowest thing between the node and reality: cognition, formalization, data, experiment, fabrication, qualification, regulation, capital, launch or adoption.
