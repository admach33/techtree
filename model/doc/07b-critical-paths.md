This section looks at every validated technology, operational system and outcome (tiers 5–7) that has reached its year and is rated High or Medium at the checkpoint. For each, it follows the latest prerequisite back at every step. That path sets the node's date. It also satisfies the framework's rule that any hardware or physical outcome forecast must have its chain written out.

**Exponential case, by 2031: {{n_cp_s2_2031}} nodes.**

- AI mathematics lies on the date-setting path for {{n_cp_ai_s2_2031}}; {{n_cp_public_s2_2031}} of those paths run through public access in 2027.
- {{n_cp_noai_crit_s2_2031}} date-setting paths have no AI-mathematics step. They run on launch, pads, propellant transfer and hardware alone, which is the two-clocks finding in its plainest form.
- {{n_cp_noai_any_s2_2031}} nodes have no AI-mathematics prerequisite anywhere. They are physical anchors that AI-math nodes depend on.
- The final binding gates are {{cp_gates_s2_2031}}.

**Exponential case, by 2035: {{n_cp_s2_2035}} nodes.**

- AI mathematics lies on the date-setting path for {{n_cp_ai_s2_2035}}; {{n_cp_public_s2_2035}} of those paths run through public access in 2027.
- {{n_cp_noai_crit_s2_2035}} date-setting paths have no AI-mathematics step.
- The final binding gates are {{cp_gates_s2_2035}}.

**Embodied case, by 2035: {{n_cp_s4_2035}} nodes.**

- AI mathematics lies on the date-setting path for {{n_cp_ai_s4_2035}}.
- Robots lie on the date-setting path for {{n_cp_robots_crit_s4_2035}}, and appear somewhere upstream of {{n_cp_robots_any_s4_2035}}.
- {{n_cp_noai_any_s4_2035}} nodes have no AI-mathematics prerequisite anywhere.
- The final binding gates are {{cp_gates_s4_2035}}.

Even by 2035, every date-setting path that robots lie on ends at a robots-branch milestone. Outside that branch robots enter only through soft dependencies: they make a node earlier or more likely, but they are never its latest hard prerequisite.
